Semi-finalists Instructions
[This is a version of the email sent to semi-finalists and will be used to add additional FAQs as they come up.]
Completing the Building Phase
1) Build your project using Kiro and the AWS Free Tier. Make sure the idea you build is based on the project idea you submitted. Your original idea submission details are in the email confirmation sent when you submitted your idea. If you don't have this, please contact Builder Support, and provide your Builder Profile alias and email used during submission.
2) Create an article about your project aligned to the article requirements and publish it on AWS Builder Center. Your article must include the required information (listed below) and be published by March 13, 2026, 8:00 PM UTC (March 13, 2026 12:00 PM PT | March 14, 2026, 4:00 AM IST). Tip: The sooner you publish, the more time you have to earn likes.
3) Earn likes for your article. You will have until March 20, 2026 8:00 PM UTC (March 20 2026, 12:00 PM PT, or March 21, 2026, 4:00 AM IST) for community voting and to collect likes on your article. The top 300 most-liked articles will then progress to the next stage. Tip: Make sure your Builder Center profile is up to date so people can learn more about you. Important: Do not create fake accounts or use bots to inflate your article likes – these articles will be disqualified.
Guidelines for creating your article
When writing your article, make sure the following sections and information is included:
1) Your article must include the following sections:
App category
List the specific category that your project aligns with: Workplace Efficiency, Daily Life Enhancement, Commercial Solutions, Social Impact, or Creative Expression
My vision
Explain what your idea is and what you built.
Why this matters
Explain why your idea matters to people. This could be a great opportunity to tie it back to the app category you selected.
How I built this
Share your technical approach, architecture, and key development milestones.
Demo
Share screen shots of your application, or create a video that is less than 5 minutes and embed it into this section. (You can upload onto YouTube and embed it using the Insert YouTube embed feature on your article.)
What I learned
Explain what you learned. Make sure to include key insights you gained as it relates to your development journey.
2) Add a cover image - something that captures attention!
3) Article Title- For your article title, use: “AIdeas: [your project or app name]”
3) Select the following tags for your article: 1) #aideas-2025, 2) your app category tag (#workplace-efficiency, #daily-life-enhancement, #commercial-solutions, #social-impact, #creative-expression) and region tag (#EMEA, #NAMER, #APJC, #LATAM, #GCR, #ANZ)
