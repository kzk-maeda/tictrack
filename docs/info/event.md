Overview
The Global 10,000 AIdeas Competition is one of the largest developer competitions in AWS history, inviting developers worldwide to build innovative AI applications using AWS tools. Participants gain hands-on experience with next-gen AI development tools, connect with innovators globally, and access technical enablement through bi-weekly workshops, AWS User Groups, and AWS Builder Center resources.
Join the competition to compete for $250,000 in cash prizes, AWS credits, and recognition from AWS across 20 winner categories. Recognition may include featured placement at AWS re:Invent 2026 and across AWS channels, as well as opportunities to connect with technical leaders.
How this works
You are tasked with pitching an application idea for one of the five competition tracks: workplace efficiency, daily life enhancement, commercial solutions, social impact, and creative expression. Your initial submission is a written pitch—no code required at this stage—that describes what you want to build and why it matters.
When joining the challenge, your submission form should clearly describe your idea, which AWS Free Tier services you'll use, and how your app will make a real difference. Keep it focused and compelling. This is about selling your vision, not building it yet.
If selected as a semi-finalist, that's when you'll build your app. Your finished app needs to use Kiro for at least part of development, stay within AWS Free Tier limits, and be completely original and not yet published. Semi-finalists will also publish a Builder Center article showcasing their app, the AWS services used, their team's name, and a demo.

Eligibility requirements
The competition is open to individuals or teams of up to four. Participants must: 1) Be 18 years or older; 2) Have a valid AWS Builder ID; 3) Use Kiro; 4) Comply with AWS Free Tier terms of service; and 5) Reside in an eligible country. Read the Terms for full details.
